Hi I'm Arvid, and every day I share
one core lesson from my Twitter
course, Find your Following.
Today, I want to talk about
unbounded Twitter growth.
A lot of people think that growing as
fast as you can to get as many followers
in as short a time as possible is the
best way to grow an audience on Twitter.
I disagree.
Slow growth, reliable growth,
trumps fast growth at all times.
Because you don't want those spikes.
You don't want thousands of
new followers all of a sudden.
The quality of the follow is often
much more important than the quantity.
If you build relationships on Twitter,
if you want long-term success,
you need long-term relationships.
And long-term relationships don't just
happen a thousand times all at once;
they happen a thousand times in a row.
So if you try to force quick
growth, you don't have the capacity
of building relationships with
the people that follow you.
You don't have the capacity
to engage with new followers.
You can't just be part of conversations
anymore if there's thousands of
conversations that you want to be part of.
Just doesn't work.
You don't have the time.
And the people who follow
you, they will notice.
They followed you; no interaction.
That is not a strong,
reliable relationship.
So don't force growth, do it slowly
because the people that you follow
and that follow you first might end up
being really good friends on Twitter.
And that is an opportunity that you
might want to look for and engage with.
Because Twitter is all
about relationships.
Relationships take time, and that time
means slower, but more reliable growth.
Don't force it .Just grow organically.
I highly recommend that
.

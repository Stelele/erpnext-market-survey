Today, I want to talk about the 80/20
rule when it comes to content on Twitter.
What I have found works really well to
build a professional brand, on Twitter;
to build a personal brand that people
really enjoy following is to talk about
the things that you are an expert in 80%
of the time, and the other things, your
personal quirks, your personal interests,
other things that you found interesting
for 20% of the time, the idea behind
this is to be highly consistent most
of the time, because when people follow
you as a thought leader or an influencer
or somebody, they actually trust,
they want you to be very
consistent because you are a
source of information to them.
You're not just entertaining them.
You're providing meaningful information.
So if they see something new from
you, they want it to be very likely
that it's something insightful
or helpful or instructive.
So if you can provide that 80% of
the time, then the remaining 20%
will be perfectly fine to talk
about food that you're cooking
or your pets, or an interesting
thing that you read somewhere else.
Doesn't really matter that much if most of
the time you provide something meaningful.
The reason for this is that once you
share everything that you want to
talk about indiscriminately, your feed
on Twitter will be very chaotic and
nobody wants to follow a chaotic feed.
So be precise.
Talk about the things that you
actually know something about 80% of
the time and keep the other things
to the 20% and people will follow you
intentionally because they know you will
provide meaningful, valuable content.

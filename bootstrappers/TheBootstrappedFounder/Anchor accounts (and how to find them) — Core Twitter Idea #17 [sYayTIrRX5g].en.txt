Following certain accounts will
impact your Twitter journey
more than following others.
Picking the right ones is essential.
Hi, my name is Arvid and every
day I share one core idea from my
Twitter course, Find your Following.
Today, I want to talk
about "anchor accounts".
These accounts are people on Twitter
who really embrace the community spirit.
These are the accounts that start
conversations, who invite in new
members from outside the community
and who really know what's going on.
They are nexus accounts.
They are connectors.
They built relationships.
Everybody knows them.
And they know a lot of people.
Usually they are podcast hosts
or journalists or otherwise
experienced experts in the field.
If you follow those accounts, you
will be consistently exposed to a
steady stream of reliable information,
great other people to follow, and
opportunities to interact with.
You should have notifications
turned on for these accounts.
Because you don't want to miss out.
Now, how do you find them?
Well, you look at who other people
are following who people like you,
your peers, the people that you want
to be in your prospective audience,
who they are currently following, and
you pick out the influential ones.
Either they have a lot of followers
already, or they are really consistent
and solid in their output: those
are the people you want to follow.
Those are the anchors and you can
usually also find them on Twitter
lists that people have curated
for experts in any given field.
So check out lists for anchor accounts.

Twitter course, Find your Following.
Today, I want to talk about time-blocking.
You can spend a lot of time on Twitter
and still not really get anywhere.
If you just read Twitter all day,
hoping for something cool to come along,
maybe engaging with it, maybe liking
a tweet, maybe retweeting it, there is
no structure to this, and you can spend
hours without doing anything meaningful.
The best way to use Twitter
professionally while having a job or
building a project is to time block it.
You need an hour max a day to
actually use Twitter effectively.
Take that hour from three to 4:00 PM
or split it up into 30 minutes in the
morning and 30 minutes in the afternoon.
That way, in-between, you can actually
do your work, but you will be able
to communicate with people both
in the morning and in the evening.
You don't need to spend hours on Twitter.
It is actually much better for your
algorithmic feed to populate with the
most interesting things if you don't
spend all of your day on Twitter.
So, use your time wisely, have a block
of time where you engage as much as you
can, where you like as much as you can
and when you retweet all the interesting
things or schedule them at the same
time in one time window in a day.

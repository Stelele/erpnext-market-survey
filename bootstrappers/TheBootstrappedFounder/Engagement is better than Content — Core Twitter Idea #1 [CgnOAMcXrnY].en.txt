Hey there, my name is Arvid and
from today on, I want to share one
core lesson for my Twitter course
"Find your Following" with you.
The very first lesson, it's also
going to be one of the most important
lessons for your whole Twitter journey.
And that is the fact that engagement
on Twitter is usually way more
effective than posting content.
It might be surprising for some people who
just see content happening on Twitter all
the time, but the most effective way of
getting people interested in who you are
and what you have to say is to interact
with them in somebody else's conversation,
you don't need to start a conversation to
participate in, but once you participate
in another person's conversation,
that can be by helping them figure
something out or providing a resource,
something that gives them an additional
perspective or just sharing your opinion.
Sometimes that can be enough to make
them way more interested in you than
any piece of content you could post.
So if you don't have much time, if you
don't have many followers to start with,
the best way to create relationships
with people where they care about
you, and what you have to say is by
interacting with them in conversations
that are happening somewhere else.
And that is tip number one, stay
tuned for the next one tomorrow.

Hey, I am Arvid and every day
I share one core lesson from my
Twitter course, Find your Following.
Today, I want to talk
about self promotion.
There are two ways that you
can self-promote on Twitter.
There's a selfish way, where you only
talk about yourself and the things you
do, and there's a selfless way, where
you talk about the things you do and who
you are in the context of your community;
in the context of other people's
challenges and helping them overcome them.
And it really makes a huge
difference because not all
self-promotion is problematic.
You should self-promote.
If you have something really interesting
to share, people should know about
it, but the way you're talking about
it makes a whole lot of difference.
The easiest rule that I try to
follow: whenever I talk about
something of mine, a book, a course
or anything, I always try to package
it in something useful, something
instructive or just entertaining.
So if you talk about what you have to
offer, put it next to something that
gives people, something meaningful,
something helpful, right now, right there.
That way, your self promotion is selfless.
At least partially: you're helping
somebody get somewhere else without
needing to buy the thing that you want
to sell, but you still put it out there
so they can buy it, if they want to.
If you only talk about yourself
without any context, people will
look at you, they see a sale or an
attempt at a sale and they won't buy.
So be selfless in your self-promotion.

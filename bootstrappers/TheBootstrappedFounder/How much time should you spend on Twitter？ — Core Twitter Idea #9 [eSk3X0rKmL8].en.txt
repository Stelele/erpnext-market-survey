Hey, my name's Arvid and everyday
I share one core lesson from my
Twitter course, Find your Following.
Today, I want to talk about how much
time you should spend on Twitter.
And I'm not sure if I'm the right person
to give you advice on this, because
I spent way too much time on Twitter
anyway, but I use it as a source of
inspiration, as a tool to find content
ideas for my newsletter and my blog and
my podcast for marketing purposes to
sell my books and to sell my courses
and for all other kinds of things.
So I guess it's okay if I spend a
little time on Twitter, but if you have
a business that is not "being a media
company on Twitter" and you actually
want to get work done, I highly recommend
that at that point, you really limit the
amount of time that you spent on Twitter.
I think you only need around
one hour max, any given day on
Twitter to use it effectively.
Make it a conscious and intentional visit.
Don't just pull up Twitter on
your lunch break because you
have five minutes to spend.
Time block somewhere in the afternoon or
in the morning to spend a dedicated 30
minutes on Twitter, doing interactions,
replying, re-tweeting, responding to
DMS,  use that time wisely, but don't
use more than an hour because the more
time you spend on Twitter, the less
time you will spend on your project.
And I think your project might
be more important than being
part of some random conversation.
So be very intentional with your time.
One hour should do it.

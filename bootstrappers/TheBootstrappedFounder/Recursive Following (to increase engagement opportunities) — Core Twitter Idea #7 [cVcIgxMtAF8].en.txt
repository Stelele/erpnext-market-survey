Hey, my name is Arvid and every

Today, I want to talk
about recursive following.
The idea here is that if you follow
somebody who you really admire,
maybe they are an expert, or maybe
they are somebody that you look up
to because they are inspirational,
check out who they are following
and who is following them.
Usually on Twitter, it's fairly easy.
You just go to this person's
profile and you check out their
follows and their followers.
And in these lists, you will
find interesting people.
They are kind of preselected
as interesting people because
they follow this person or they
are followed by this person.
So by going through those lists, you
can pick and choose all these other
interesting people that might provide
meaningful additional value to your
feed, if you would choose to follow them.
And then you follow those people.
And then again, you check their
follows and their followers.
And in those lists you find, again, a
preselected list of interesting follows.
The idea is that we're all connected in
really interesting networks, but these
networks are usually closed graphs.
They are highly interconnected.
So by finding people who are connected
to this person that you already
like, the chance is that you will
like these other people as well.
So in connecting with people
that are pre-connected, you can
recursively, step-by-step go through
the whole graph and figure out the
best people to follow on Twitter.
And once you follow those people, you
will be exposed to their conversations.
And once you're exposed to those
conversations, you can participate
and invite other people from
their audience to follow you.
So find the best people, recursively,
find the other great people in their
following and do that for as long as
it takes for you to build a really
nice group of people to follow.
And then you can audition
in front of that audience.

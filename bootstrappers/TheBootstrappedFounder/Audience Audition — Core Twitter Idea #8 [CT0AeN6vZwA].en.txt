about the audience audition.
So when you start out on Twitter
and you have no followers at all,
or just a couple, it really doesn't
make sense to tweet into the void.
But what you can do to find interesting
people that might want to follow you
is to go to where already ongoing
conversations are happening and
participate in front of all those people.
Because if you follow somebody who has
a couple thousand followers and they
start a conversation about any particular
topic, you can be pretty sure that
some of these thousands of followers
will participate in the conversation.
So if you participate in somebody
else's conversation, you don't start
it, you just participate, then you
are essentially acting in front of all
these followers that this person has.
So you audition in
front of their audience.
You don't audition in front of the
person with all these followers; you
audition in front of their followers.
And the consequence is that you
are essentially participating in a
conversation in front of people who
are just like you, because just like
you, they follow this person; just
like you, they see this conversation.
So you're really pre-selecting a
great audience in front of what
you can audition for yourself.
You can share that you are a great
person, you are helpful, you're
motivating, you're supportive and you
can do this in front of all these other
people that are not following you yet.
But they might be if you
play your cards right.
So that's how audience audition works.
You find people with a large following,
you look at those conversations
and if you have something to
contribute, you do that and therefore
audition in front of this audience.
Now you have to be really helpful for
people to see you and to click your
profile and then eventually follow you.
You have to provide something meaningful.
Just participating and commenting
something is not going to be enough.
So be helpful, contribute and give people
something that attracts them to you.

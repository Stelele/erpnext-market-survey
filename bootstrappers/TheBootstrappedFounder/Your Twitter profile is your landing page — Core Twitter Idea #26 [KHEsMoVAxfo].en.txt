Hey, my name is Arvid and every

Today, I want to talk about the
importance of your Twitter profile.
Your avatar, your description,
your bio, your header, all of that
is effectively the landing page
for you as a person on Twitter.
And if you treat it like a business
would treat their landing page, you
would optimize it for conversion, right?
You would try to figure out how
to  convey with it, what you are
about, what are you going to do?
Where do you come from?
What have you said in the past?
All of this is in your Twitter profile.
And if you can arrange these things
in a way that attracts people into
your brand, into your following,
then you should focus on this.
You should find a really nice avatar
picture that is friendly, that
invites people into a conversation.
You should pick a name that people can
understand and remember, you should
write a bio that expresses who you
are, where your credibility comes
from, what are you going to do in the
future, what you've done in the past.
All of this is in that one page.
So be sure to optimize this, to be as
clear as you can, because the Twitter
bio is a big part in the follower
funnel: people find you somewhere
with a tweet and then they come to
your bio and then they make a choice.
Will I click the follow button?
Or will I not?
And if your bio  promises a lot of good
things, and it shows that you've been
reliably doing these things in the past
then people will follow you and then you
can build this honest and trust-based
relationship with them from day one.
So treat your Twitter bio like a business
would treat their landing page and then
make it about promising them what you're
going to be doing and stick to it.

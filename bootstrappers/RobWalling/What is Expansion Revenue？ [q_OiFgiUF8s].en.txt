what is expansion Revenue the
opportunity for a customer to pay you
more money as they get more value from
your product so in essence it's having
pricing tiers that auto upgrade folks as
they get more value and this is great
because you make more money the first
order effect is you make more money the
second order effect is that you can
literally add zero customers to your
business in a given month but if you
have expansion revenue and your churn is
low enough you can have what's called
net negative churn this is where you add
zero customers and your business still
grows the best and fastest growing SAS
companies in the world have expansion
Revenue that leads to net negative churn

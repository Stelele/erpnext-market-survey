## Content Moat Analysis

### Strongest Moats (Hard to Replicate)
1. **Version-specific deep dives** — Requires staying current, testing, documenting
2. **Accounting workflow expertise** — Requires both accounting + ERPNext knowledge
3. **Integration complexity** — SSO setups are always painful, always needed
4. **Industry-specific solutions** — Requires domain expertise (hospitality, lending)

### Weaker Moats (Easy to Replicate)
1. **Basic tutorials** — Already saturated by community
2. **Quick fixes** — Stack Overflow answers these
3. **News/updates** — Better covered by ERPNext blog

## Partner-Led Growth Opportunities

1. **Partner Recruitment Funnel**: Build a structured process to convert active forum freelancers/hiring posts into formal partners — provide a landing page, partner tier benefits, and a simple onboarding flow. Target: the 52+ freelancer posters per year.

2. **Partner Co-Marketing**: Recruit partners with vertical solutions (Garment/RMG, Manufacturing, Logistics) for joint lead generation. Provide them with a partner kit (presentations, case studies, trial accounts) in exchange for referral agreements.

3. **Referral Partner Program**: Offer 10-20% first-year commission to anyone who refers paid ERPNext implementations. No technical requirements — pure referral. Works for consultants, ERP bloggers, industry influencers.

4. **White-Label Reseller Program**: Allow partners to rebrand basic ERPNext configurations (industry templates, pre-configured workflows) as their own product. Charge a platform fee + margin on transactions.

5. **Partner Q&A Authority**: Establish yourself as the partner community's knowledge hub — answer implementation questions in the forum, build partner-facing documentation, and create a partner newsletter. Converts visibility into recruitment leverage.

## Direct Sales Required Warnings

These opportunities require direct sales capability and should not be attempted through passive partner channels alone:

1. **Enterprise ERPNext Customization** (Topic 159231: Ukrainian/Russian developer for manufacturing; Topic 161245: Nepal jute manufacturing consultant) — enterprise deals need account management, not just partner referrals.

2. **Multi-site / Multi-company ERPNext deployments** — complex rollouts require direct project management and senior consultant involvement.

3. **ERPNext-to-ERPNext integration projects** (Topic 157079) — integration work is specialist and needs direct technical sales.

4. **Gold/Silver partner recruitment** — tier-1 partners require a formal partnership pitch, legal agreements, and revenue commitments. Not a self-serve channel.

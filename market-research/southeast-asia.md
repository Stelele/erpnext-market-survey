## Country-by-Country Analysis

### Singapore — Most Developed (OPP-SEA-01)
**Most mature SEA market for ERPNext**

- English-speaking (easiest localization)
- IRAS (Inland Revenue Authority) compliance
- Singapore GST 9% handling
- Xero and QuickBooks strong competitors
- SAP and Oracle in enterprise

**What's needed:**
- IRAS-compliant tax reporting
- Singapore GST filing
- Singapore chart of accounts
- Corppass integration (government digital services)

**GitHub:** `agile-technica/erpnext-indonesia-localization` (40 stars) — Indonesia focus, not Singapore

---

### Malaysia — High Potential (OPP-SEA-02)
**Growing ERPNext community, SST compliance**

- Malaysian SST (Sales and Service Tax): 6% standard rate, 0% exempt
- LHDN e-invoicing mandate approaching (similar to Europe's Peppol)
- MyInvois (Malaysian e-invoicing system by LHDN) — timeline approaching
- Dominant software: SQL Accounting, MYOB, pastel

**What's needed:**
- LHDN e-invoicing integration (MyInvois API)
- Malaysian SST calculation and reporting
- Malaysian chart of accounts
- Malaysian company registration formats (SSM)

**GitHub:** No Malaysia-specific ERPNext repos found

## SEA Regional Opportunities

### OPP-SEA-07: Multi-Country SEA Payroll
**Priority: MEDIUM** | **Confidence: 7/10**

Each SEA country has unique payroll requirements:
- Singapore: CPF (Central Provident Fund)
- Malaysia: EPF (Employees' Provident Fund), SOCSO, EIS
- Vietnam: Social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance
- Indonesia: Jamsostek/BPJS
- Thailand: Social security
- Philippines: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG

**What's needed:** ERPNext payroll module with per-country SEA configurations.

---

### OPP-SEA-08: ASEAN E-Invoice Interoperability
**Priority: MEDIUM** | **Confidence: 6/10**

ASEAN countries are moving toward Peppol-style e-invoicing standards. Cross-border trade needs compatible invoice formats.

**What's needed:** Peppol-compliant e-invoice format for ERPNext covering SEA country requirements.

# Slow Burn Marketer

**Persona archetype:** The founder who builds audiences over years through consistent, valuable content. Rejects the "overnight success" narrative. Treats content creation as compound interest — each piece of content builds on the last, creating a moat that's hard for competitors to replicate quickly.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "The community piece was more about connecting with people than selling to them." — Daniel Vassallo

> "I don't need to be the best. I just need to be good enough for a specific group of people." — Daniel Vassallo

> "Twitter for me is an inspiration generator. Every morning I find something that sparks a new idea." — Daniel Vassallo

## Discovery Triggers

- Multi-year consistent content creation (blog, newsletter, YouTube, podcast)
- Diversified content formats: blog post → Twitter thread → YouTube video → course → book
- Treating content as a distribution channel, not just a marketing tool
- Building community around content (Slack, Discord, email list)
- Guest speakers and collaborative content as community infrastructure
- Patience for content investments that take years to pay off

## Validation Threshold

- Consistent audience growth over 12+ months (not viral spikes)
- Email list or community of engaged followers who opt in over time
- Content generates inbound leads without cold outreach
- Content topics reveal clear niche expertise positioning
- Audience trust established before any monetization attempt

## Red Flags

- Inconsistent content creation (feast or famine publishing)
- Chasing trending topics rather than building coherent expertise
- Monetizing too early before trust is established
- Conflating follower count with an engaged audience
- Ignoring metrics that matter (email open rates, content engagement) for vanity metrics (followers)

- Run a long-term ERPNext-focused blog (2-3 posts/week for 2+ years)
- Build an ERPNext YouTube channel with tutorials, case studies, and comparisons

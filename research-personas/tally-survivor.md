# Persona: Tally Survivor

**Source:** Non-English Market Research — India
**Read:** market-research/india.md

---

## Profile

The Tally Survivor is an Indian accountant or business owner who has used Tally (the dominant accounting software in India, ~10M+ users) for 5-20 years. They know Tally inside and out — every shortcut, every quirk, every workaround. They are deeply resistant to switching but increasingly frustrated.

## Core Characteristics

**What they believe:**
- Tally is the only "real" accounting software
- ERPNext is for tech companies, not "real" businesses
- Switching would take months and cost a fortune
- Their staff will never learn a new system

**What actually frustrates them:**
- Tally Prime subscription price hikes (moving to SaaS model)
- Inability to manage multi-state operations with separate GST registrations
- Poor inventory management for trading/manufacturing
- Limited reporting and business intelligence
- No mobile access
- Can't handle complex workflows

**What they secretly want:**
- Modern ERP capabilities without abandoning their Tally familiarity
- Multi-GSTIN management
- Better inventory and manufacturing features
- Cloud access and mobile
- Reports they can actually use

## How They Evaluate Opportunities

**Adoption blocker signals they look for:**
- "Works like Tally" / familiar UX
- Can import their existing Tally data
- Hindi interface option
- Local support available

**Red flags that kill interest:**
- "Cloud-first" without understanding Indian internet constraints
- Only English interface
- Complex setup requirements
- Requires hiring a developer to maintain

**Pricing tolerance:**
- $300-2,000 one-time for software
- $50-200/month for SaaS
- Will not pay for "implementation hours" at developer rates

## Tactics They Use

- Trial on personal computer first, not production
- Demand data migration work themselves (don't trust consultants)
- Ask for Hindi/regional language support first
- Compare against Tally Prime pricing explicitly
- Want references from other Indian businesses

## WTP Signals

- Topic 83499: 4,789 views on broken Tally migration tool
- Active freelance projects on ERPNext India Telegram channels
- Hybrowlabs paid courses got traction (proves training market exists)
- ERPgulf Saudi seed data proves regional demand

## How to Win Them Over

1. **Build a working Tally migrator** — this is the #1 unlock. If you solve the data migration problem, everything else follows.
2. **Show Tally-style reports** — Indian accountants love the Tally report format. Replicate it in ERPNext.
3. **Offer Hindi interface** — even partial Hindi support is a differentiator.
4. **Demo with Indian company data** — not "Apple and Samsung." Use realistic Indian business data.
5. **Price against Tally Prime** — show the annual cost comparison explicitly.

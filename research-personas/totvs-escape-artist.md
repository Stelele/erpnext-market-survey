# Persona: Totvs Escape Artist

**Source:** Non-English Market Research — Brazil
**Read:** market-research/brazil.md

---

## Profile

The Totvs Escape Artist is a Brazilian business owner or IT manager currently trapped in Totvs — the dominant Brazilian ERP with ~50%+ market share. They are paying massive licensing fees (R$5,000-50,000+/month) and feel locked in. They desperately want out but can't find a viable exit.

## Core Characteristics

**What they pay today:**
- Totvs licensing: R$5,000-50,000+/month
- Implementation partner fees for customizations
- Annual maintenance fees
- Tax compliance add-ons
- Per-user fees that scale with growth

**What they hate about Totvs:**
- Extremely expensive customizations (only Totvs partners can touch the code)
- Slow to add features
- Forced to accept Totvs's roadmap
- Vendor lock-in at its worst
- Can't integrate with modern tools easily

**What they secretly want:**
- Modern, customizable ERP without lock-in
- Open-source flexibility
- Ability to hire any developer, not just Totvs-certified partners
- Pricing that scales fairly
- Freedom

## Why They Can't Escape (Yet)

ERPNext cannot replace Totvs without:
1. **NF-e emission** — legally required for any Brazilian company
2. **Tax calculation** — can't compute ICMS, PIS, COFINS
3. **SPED export** — mandatory for most companies

Without these, Totvs Escape Artists are stuck. They know ERPNext is better, but compliance requirements make the switch impossible.

## How They Evaluate Opportunities

**What they look for:**
- "100% Brazilian compliance" — they need this before anything else
- Clear roadmap to full Totvs replacement
- Brazilian implementation partner who knows both systems
- Real migration path, not just a demo

**Red flags:**
- "ERPNext is like Totvs but cheaper" (dangerously wrong)
- No Brazilian compliance mentioned
- English-speaking support only
- No Totvs migration experience

**Pricing tolerance:**
- Will pay R$10,000-100,000 for a proper migration
- Monthly costs should be 50-70% less than Totvs
- Will pay for certainty that compliance works

## The Unlock

Build NF-e emission (the #1 blocker). Totvs Escape Artists will line up. The migration consulting revenue alone (R$10,000-50,000 per company) funds the compliance module development.

The strategy:
1. Build NF-e emission
2. Target Totvs customers paying R$10,000+/month
3. Offer migration + 70% cost reduction
4. First 10 customers fund the next compliance module

- TechMax Soluções with 48 repos, SPED focus
- High Totvs pricing driving migration interest
- SAP, Senior, Sankhya customers also potential targets

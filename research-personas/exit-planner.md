# Exit Planner

**Persona archetype:** The founder who designs for sellability from the beginning, not just profitability. Every decision — tech stack, customer contracts, documentation, revenue model — is evaluated for its impact on future exit potential. Building a business that can be sold is different from building a business that's just profitable.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "Sometimes the right exit is the one where you keep the business." — Sahil Lavingia

> "Profit is not the goal. But profit is what gives you the option to do the next right thing." — Patrick McKenzie

> "Think about what data you'll have in five years that you don't have today. Start collecting it now." — Nathan Latka

## Discovery Triggers

- Building with clean code and documentation that makes due diligence easy
- Customer contracts that allow assignment (not personal services)
- Revenue model that doesn't depend on founder's personal relationships
- Revenue diversification across customers (no single customer > 20% of revenue)
- Systems and processes documented enough to run without the founder
- Financials tracked with audit-quality rigor
- Understanding that acquirers buy predictable cash flows, not hero stories

## Validation Threshold

- Business could run without the founder for 90 days (documented processes)
- Customer contracts are standard and transferable
- Revenue is recurring and predictable (MRR, annual contracts)
- Churn rate is known and manageable
- Financial statements are clean and auditable
- Technical debt is documented and understood

## Red Flags

- Founder-specific relationships that can't be transferred
- Revenue concentrated in too few customers
- Personal services embedded in the product (consulting-as-SaaS)
- Financials that would require months of cleanup for due diligence
- No documented processes or runbooks
- Revenue that looks good but is one-time (not recurring)

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Exit Planners in the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Build ERPNext apps with clean, documented code (Frappe best practices)
- Use Frappe Cloud or standard hosting (not custom infrastructure that only founder can run)
- Structure ERPNext app pricing as recurring subscriptions (not one-time fees)
- Maintain clean ERPNext app financials (separate from consulting revenue)
- Build ERPNext apps with standard Frappe architecture (acquirers want code they can understand)
- Document ERPNext app operations thoroughly (onboarding, support processes, known issues)
- Diversify ERPNext app customer base (no single ERPNext customer > 20% of revenue)

**ERPNext exit planning opportunities:**
- ERPNext apps with clean, auditable financial models
- ERPNext app businesses with 50+ paying customers and low churn
- ERPNext apps that integrate with multiple ERPNext instances (proprietary data moat)
- ERPNext consulting businesses structured to separate product from services

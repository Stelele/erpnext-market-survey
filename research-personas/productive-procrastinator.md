# Productive Procrastinator

**Persona archetype:** The founder who has made peace with their procrastination — not by fighting it, but by channeling it. Procrastination energy gets redirected into adjacent high-impact work rather than the dreaded task. The result: the avoided task gets done eventually, but a dozen valuable side projects emerge from the sidetracking.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "I don't procrastinate. I allocate my procrastination budget to things I should probably be doing anyway." — (The Bootstrapped Founder #354)

## Discovery Triggers

- Founders who describe procrastination as a feature, not a bug
- Building tools or systems during "procrastination sessions" that become real products
- Using procrastination as a signal — if the founder is procrastinating on a task, maybe it doesn't need to be done
- Side projects that emerge from procrastination energy and turn into revenue streams
- Comfortable with shipping imperfect work and iterating later
- Distinguishing between avoidance (fear) and redirection (strategic energy allocation)

## Validation Threshold

- High output volume despite unconventional work patterns
- Multiple shipped products from procrastination sidetracks
- The avoided primary task eventually gets done (or is legitimately unnecessary)
- Work quality doesn't suffer — just the work schedule

## Red Flags

- Using "productive procrastination" as an excuse for never doing hard things
- High number of half-finished projects with no completed launches
- Procrastination that's actually avoidance of critical decisions or difficult conversations
- Shipped products of poor quality due to rushing to finish procrastination-derived work

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Productive Procrastinators in the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Build ERPNext apps as "procrastination projects" that turn into real products
- Use ERPNext development as creative outlet while avoiding more tedious tasks
- Create ERPNext automation scripts that solve their own ERPNext pain points
- Build ERPNext community tools during procrastination sessions
- Ship ERPNext apps quickly without perfectionism, iterating based on feedback

**ERPNext procrastination opportunities:**
- ERPNext apps born from avoiding other ERPNext work

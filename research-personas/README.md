# Research Personas: Finding Paid Pain in the ERPNext Ecosystem

A structured library of bootstrapped founder methodologies, extracted from the MicroConf, Rob Walling, and The Bootstrapped Founder transcript corpus. Each persona is an analytical lens for identifying paid pain points that could become ERPNext ecosystem opportunities.

## How to Use These Personas

### For Problem Discovery

Read each persona's **Discovery Triggers** section. These are the signals each founder methodology pays attention to. Map them to the ERPNext ecosystem:

- What are ERPNext users complaining about repeatedly?
- What ERPNext workflows are people stitching together manually?
- What ERPNext integrations is the community requesting on the Frappe Forum?
- What regulatory or macro changes create new ERPNext compliance needs?

### For Solution Validation

Read each persona's **Validation Threshold** and **Red Flags** sections. Before committing to an ERPNext opportunity, pressure-test it against the methodology most relevant to your target customer.

### For ERPNext Ecosystem Mapping

Each persona includes an **ERPNext Ecosystem Lens** section with specific opportunity areas within ERPNext.

## Methodology

Personas were extracted through:

1. Full transcript analysis of priority episodes (Pieter Levels TBF, Daniel Vassallo TBF, Arvid Kahl constraints/moat/procrastination/scraping/virality/validation/vanity series, Rob Walling 16 Lessons)
2. `summaries.md` structured analysis of 16 transcripts
3. Pattern identification across transcripts for subtle personas beyond the original 9 confirmed
4. Cross-referencing discovery triggers and validation thresholds against ERPNext ecosystem specifics

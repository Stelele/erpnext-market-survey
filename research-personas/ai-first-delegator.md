# AI-First Delegator

**Persona archetype:** The founder who treats AI as a first-call collaborator — for ideation, code drafting, customer communication, and execution. The founder's role is taste, judgment, and direction. AI handles the first pass at everything. Speed and taste replace effort and hours as the primary competitive advantages.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "You don't need to be a machine. You need to be the person who knows how to talk to the machine." — Dr. Rumman Chowdhury

> "The solo preneur advantage is speed and taste — AI gives you speed; you provide the taste." — Dr. Rumman Chowdhury

> "AI is a collaborator, not a consultant. You have to push back on it." — Dr. Rumman Chowdhury

## Discovery Triggers

- Using AI for first drafts of landing pages, emails, documentation — human edits for voice
- Using AI for code scaffolding, debugging, and boilerplate — ship faster
- Using AI to synthesize customer feedback and spot patterns in support tickets
- Prompt chaining: breaking complex tasks into a chain of prompts
- Setting a "quality bar" before prompting to evaluate AI output without bias
- Building in public with AI: treating AI output as drafts to be refined

## Validation Threshold

- Shipping velocity significantly higher than industry average for solo founders
- AI output quality is consistently high (founder has strong taste to filter)
- AI handles 80% of the work in 20% of the tasks — identifying the right tasks for AI delegation
- Cost of AI tools is a fraction of the value produced (low overhead)

## Red Flags

- Spending more time prompting and editing AI output than it would take to just do the work
- AI-generated content that sounds robotic or lacks authentic founder voice
- Over-relying on AI for judgment calls that require deep customer understanding
- Using AI without understanding how it works — treating it as a black box
- Missing quality control — shipping AI output without human review

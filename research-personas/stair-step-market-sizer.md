# Stair-Step Market Sizer

**Persona archetype:** The founder who validates market size by literally paying rent to the market before building. Each "step" is a paid commitment that tests the next level of scale — consulting → productized service → software → scale. Never skips steps. Never invests more than the current step has validated.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "Start with an audience, not a product. Build an audience in your target niche before you have a product. The product is the monetization of the audience." — Pawel Brzeminski

> "Your first 10 customers will teach you more than any market research." — Pawel Brzeminski

> "The best SaaS ideas come from watching real people struggle with real problems for months before you build anything." — Rob Walling

## Discovery Triggers

- Founders who start as consultants/solopreneurs in a niche before building software
- Products that emerge from repeated client work (built the same thing 3 times → productize it)
- Audiences built before products (newsletter, blog, YouTube channel as market validation)
- Sequential validation: consulting → productized consulting → fixed-price service → SaaS
- Industry-specific expertise that creates natural market entry barriers
- Geographic or regulatory specialization that limits competition

## Validation Threshold

- Consulting revenue proves willingness to pay and reveals exact pain points
- Repeated client requests for the same solution indicate productization potential
- Audience size (newsletter subscribers, YouTube views) indicates market interest
- Clearest signal: clients asking "Can you make this available to my colleagues/clients?"

## Red Flags

- Building software without consulting or audience precedent
- Market size estimates based on top-down TAM/SAM rather than bottom-up customer evidence
- Ignoring signals from early customers about what they actually need
- Skipping from idea → SaaS without validating with paid alternatives first
- Choosing niches based on personal interest rather than demonstrated market demand

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Stair-Step Market Sizers targeting ERPNext would follow a clear progression:

**Step 1 (Consulting):** Become an ERPNext implementation specialist. Charge $75-150/hr for ERPNext setup, customization, data migration. Learn what companies struggle with most.

**Step 2 (Productized Service):** Package repeated consulting deliverables into fixed-price offerings — ERPNext migration from QuickBooks ($2,500 flat), ERPNext inventory setup for manufacturing ($1,500 flat), ERPNext GST compliance configuration ($999 flat).

**Step 3 (Software):** Productize the most-requested service into an ERPNext app — the GST compliance tool becomes a standalone app, the migration tool becomes a guided migration wizard.

**Step 4 (Scale):** Recurring SaaS revenue from the app, with consulting as a premium upsell for complex implementations.

**ERPNext stair-step opportunities:**
- ERPNext training as the audience-building step (courses, workshops, YouTube tutorials)
- ERPNext partner certification creating credibility for paid offerings
- ERPNext vertical templates as productized services before app products
- ERPNext community membership as the audience layer before premium tools

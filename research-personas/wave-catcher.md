# Wave Catcher

**Persona archetype:** The founder who spots and rides macroeconomic, technological, or cultural shifts before they become obvious. Not a trend-hopper, but a pattern-recognizer who identifies structural changes in how people work, companies operate, or industries transform — then builds for the early majority of that wave.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "The biggest opportunity in 2025 is building software for the growing gap between small business needs and what legacy enterprise tools can serve — the 'forgotten middle' market." — Andy Baldacci

> "The biggest companies in the world will never care about the five-person business. That's your beachhead." — Andy Baldacci

> "The founders who will win aren't the ones with the most tools. They're the ones who know which tool to use for which problem." — Dr. Rumman Chowdhury

## Discovery Triggers

- Identifying underserved market segments between enterprise and consumer (the "forgotten middle")
- Building for trigger events: new hires, new regulations, new software adoption, industry disruptions
- Paying attention to regulatory changes that create new compliance requirements (and new software needs)
- Recognizing when legacy tools become abandonware — no longer supported but still widely used
- Spotting when a demographic or economic shift creates new operational challenges (remote work, inflation, new tax rules)
- AI adoption waves: identifying which roles/functions AI augments first in specific industries

## Validation Threshold

- Clear trigger event identified that forces behavior change (companies must act, not just want to)
- Early majority adoption has begun but the market is still fragmented enough for bootstrappers
- Willingness to pay is clear (compliance fines, efficiency losses, or competitive pressure)
- Timing is not too early (paying for something that isn't urgent yet) and not too late ( incumbents already dominate)

## Red Flags

- Riding every shiny new trend without distinguishing signal from noise
- Building for a shift that's "five years away" — too early for paying customers today
- Ignoring incumbents who will respond to the same trend with more resources
- Conflating personal enthusiasm for a trend with market readiness
- Failing to identify the specific trigger event that will drive adoption

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Wave Catchers in the ERPNext ecosystem would identify macro shifts affecting ERPNext users:

**Current high-probability waves:**
1. **AI integration wave:** ERPNext users need AI-assisted reporting, prediction, and automation — but must remain affordable and controllable
2. **SMB compliance wave:** New countries implementing GST, VAT, or digital tax requirements that ERPNext must support — ERPNext apps filling compliance gaps
3. **Remote/distributed work wave:** ERPNext users managing geographically distributed teams need better mobile, async, and multi-timezone features
4. **Data sovereignty wave:** Companies needing ERPNext deployed in specific geographic regions for data residency compliance
5. **ERP migration wave:** Companies abandoning SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks for affordable alternatives — ERPNext as the landing spot
6. **Sustainability/reporting wave:** ESG reporting requirements creating new data collection needs in ERPNext

**ERPNext wave-catching opportunities:**
- ERPNext AI copilot apps (financial forecasting, inventory prediction, anomaly detection)
- Country-specific ERPNext compliance packs (Nigeria, Kenya, Philippines tax/legal requirements)
- ERPNext carbon footprint tracking modules
- ERPNext multi-tenant deployment tools for hosting companies
- ERPNext API gateway and analytics for companies connecting ERPNext to BI tools

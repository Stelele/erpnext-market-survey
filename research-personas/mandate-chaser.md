# Persona: Mandate Chaser

**Source:** Non-English Market Research — Africa (generalized)
**Read:** market-research/africa.md

---

## Profile

The Mandate Chaser is a consultant, developer, or entrepreneur who builds compliance solutions BEFORE regulatory deadlines hit. They monitor government tax authorities across multiple countries, identify upcoming mandates, and race to build solutions before the deadline. They are the opposite of the Deadline Fighter — proactive where others are reactive.

## Core Characteristics

**What they do:**
- Monitor FIRS (Nigeria), KRA (Kenya), SARS (South Africa), DGCCIQ (Morocco), ZATCA (Saudi), SAT (Mexico), SEFAZ (Brazil)
- Track e-invoicing mandates across Africa, MENA, and LatAm
- Identify countries where ERPNext is NOT compliant with upcoming mandates
- Build compliance modules in advance of deadlines
- Partner with local compliance experts

**Their advantage:**
- First mover in compliance-underserved markets
- Pre-built solutions ready when deadline hits
- Trusted relationships with local tax authorities
- Network effect across countries

**Their weakness:**
- Needs to build before WTP is proven
- Investment risk if deadline shifts
- Requires local compliance expertise in each country

## The Strategy

**Phase 1 — Monitor:**
- Track all African e-invoicing mandates
- Map ERPNext readiness per country
- Identify gaps vs. upcoming deadlines

**Phase 2 — Prioritize:**
- Rank countries by: deadline urgency × ERPNext gap × market size
- Nigeria FIRS: CRITICAL (passed with no solution)
- Kenya KRA: HIGH (upcoming mandate)
- Saudi ZATCA Phase 3: ACTIVE (July 2025)
- Morocco DGCCIQ: HIGH (e-invoice mandate active)

**Phase 3 — Build:**
- Partner with local compliance expert (accountant, tax lawyer)
- Build compliance module for highest-priority country
- Get solution live BEFORE deadline
- Use as reference for next market

**Phase 4 — Expand:**
- Leverage trust and relationships to expand to neighboring countries
- Build regional compliance expertise
- Create recurring compliance maintenance revenue

## How They Evaluate Opportunities

**What they look for:**
- Countries with CLEAR upcoming mandates (not speculative)
- ERPNext has SIGNIFICANT gap (not just polish work)
- Local partner available who understands compliance
- First mover advantage still exists

**Red flags:**
- "Everyone needs this" (no specific mandate)
- Country with no clear deadline
- ERPNext already compliant
- No local expertise available

**Pricing model:**
- $5,000-20,000 per country implementation
- $500-1,500/month per company for compliance maintenance
- Premium pricing during crisis window (1-2 months before deadline)

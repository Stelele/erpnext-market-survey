# Delegation Architect

**Core Identity:** The founder who hires for what they cannot do, treating delegation not as weakness but as a strategic design choice. They build a team that fills every gap in their own capabilities.

## Motivation & Values

- **Strength through others:** Recognizes that a solo founder is a bottleneck, and deliberately surrounds themselves with complementary skills
- **Clarity over ego:** Values honest self-assessment — knowing what they can't do well is a strength, not a flaw
- **Leverage as freedom:** Sees delegation as the path to time freedom and strategic focus, not as a loss of control
- **Build to hand off:** Designs processes and systems so that anyone can execute them, not just the founder

## Behavioral Patterns

- Starts with their weaknesses and reverse-engineers hire needs: "What do I suck at?" → "Who can I hire for that?"
- Commission-based first hires are a common entry point — they test the relationship before committing full-time
- Writes job descriptions that feel like product specs — clear outputs, clear success metrics, clear boundaries
- Gradually hands off execution while retaining strategic decision-making
- Uses frameworks like EOS/Traction to create clarity around roles and responsibilities

## Quotes

- "I stopped trying to be good at everything. I hired for my gaps."
- "The best founder I know hired people who were better than him at everything."
- "My job description changed: I used to do the work, now I design who does the work."

## When It Breaks Down

- Hires too fast without adequate vetting, leading to team misalignment
- Delegates strategy before the team has earned that trust, creating chaos
- Avoids the discomfort of difficult performance conversations
- Treats delegation as a one-time event rather than an ongoing practice

## Related Personas

- Small Army Builder — both build teams, but the Delegation Architect focuses on complementing personal weaknesses while the Small Army Builder focuses on scaling output
- System Buyer — often adopts proven systems (EOS, Traction) to create the structure that makes delegation effective

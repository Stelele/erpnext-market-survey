# Calm Biz Rationalist

**Persona archetype:** The founder who treats building a business like managing a system — measured, deliberate, emotionally steady. Rejects the startup hype machine. Tracks runway, burn rate, and cohort revenue religiously. Profitability is a standard to maintain, not a milestone to celebrate once.

## Discovery Triggers

- Ruthless financial tracking: weekly burn rate and runway calculations
- Infrastructure cost obsession: keeping server/CDN/database costs under 10% of revenue
- Open-source infrastructure stack (PostgreSQL, Redis, Fly.io) to minimize costs
- Usage-based or consumption-based pricing to align customer value with revenue
- Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with top customers to catch churn risks early
- Revenue cohorts tracked religiously — new cohorts must grow faster than old cohorts churn

## Validation Threshold

- Business is cash-flow positive without relying on outside investment
- Unit economics are healthy: CAC < LTV, gross margins > 70%
- Runway measured in months is known at all times
- Annual contracts or high retained revenue indicate customer success
- Cohort revenue per user increases over time (expansion revenue, upsells)

## Red Flags

- Not knowing monthly burn rate or runway
- Customer acquisition cost higher than first-year revenue per customer (negative payback)
- Gross margins below 50% in SaaS (infrastructure + COGS consuming too much)
- Churn rate above 8% monthly (unsustainable without constant acquisition pressure)
- Ignoring unit economics in favor of "we'll figure it out at scale"

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Calm Biz Rationalists in the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Build ERPNext apps with minimal server costs (use Frappe Cloud or managed hosting to avoid DevOps overhead)
- Target ERPNext consulting as high-margin work ($100+/hr) that funds app development
- Keep ERPNext app infrastructure costs low (use Frappe's existing infrastructure where possible)
- Track ERPNext app metrics: active installs, renewal rates, support ticket volume
- Build ERPNext tools that reduce their own support burden (self-service onboarding, automated documentation)
- Use ERPNext itself to run the business (use the product, not just build for others)

**High-value ERPNext rationalist opportunities:**
- ERPNext cost optimization tools (identify wasted resources, suggest workflow improvements)
- ERPNext billing reconciliation apps (auto-detect billing discrepancies)
- ERPNext subscription management for companies running multiple ERPNext instances
- ERPNext audit trail and compliance dashboards for financial visibility
- ERPNext ROI calculators that quantify the value of ERPNext implementation

# Persona: Arabic Digital Transformer

**Source:** Non-English Market Research — MENA
**Read:** market-research/mena.md

---

## Profile

The Arabic Digital Transformer is a business owner, CFO, or IT manager in the Middle East or North Africa who is moving from manual systems, legacy ERP, or international ERP to ERPNext. They need full Arabic interface, RTL (right-to-left) support, and local compliance — not a Western product with a half-finished Arabic translation.

## Core Characteristics

**What they believe:**
- ERPNext can work for their business if properly localized
- Arabic-first interface is non-negotiable, not optional
- Local compliance (VAT, e-invoicing) must be built-in
- They deserve the same quality as English-language ERPNext

**What actually frustrates them:**
- ERPNext's Arabic translation is incomplete
- RTL print layouts break in unexpected ways
- Arabic accounting terminology is inconsistent
- Many fields still show English labels
- Arabic date and number formatting is wrong

**What they want:**
- Professional Arabic UI throughout ERPNext
- RTL print formats that actually work
- Arabic document templates (invoices, receipts, reports)
- Arabic customer support
- Local compliance (Saudi ZATCA, UAE VAT, Egypt)

## The RTL Challenge

Right-to-left interface isn't just flipping text. It's:
- Mirrored layouts (sidebar on right, content on left)
- RTL-aware CSS and print templates
- Bidirectional text handling (numbers and English words in Arabic text)
- Arabic-specific pagination and reading flow
- Currency and number formatting (Arabic numerals vs. Western)

Most ERPNext "Arabic support" stops at basic translation. The RTL print/layout problem is barely touched.

## How They Evaluate Opportunities

**What they look for:**
- Full Arabic UI throughout ERPNext (not just menu items)
- RTL print templates that mirror correctly
- Arabic accounting terminology consistency
- ZATCA-compliant invoices for Saudi (Fatoora QR code)
- Local tax compliance built in

**Red flags:**
- "Arabic is supported" without specifics
- English labels still appearing throughout
- Print preview showing broken RTL layouts
- No Arabic-speaking support available
- Saudi/ZATCA compliance not addressed

**Pricing tolerance:**
- $200-500/month for properly localized solution
- $1,000-5,000 for custom Arabic print suite
- Will pay premium for professional Arabic-first product

**Arabic RTL Print Studio:**

# Small Bets Statistician

**Persona archetype:** The founder who rejects the all-or-nothing bet in favor of a diversified portfolio of small, independent revenue streams. Probability of overall success is maintained while variance is dramatically reduced. Ships fast, validates with real money, moves on if no signal.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "Instead of one product that could make $1M or $0, build 10 products that each could make $100K or $0. Expected value is similar, variance is dramatically lower." — Daniel Vassallo

> "If I were to lose $5,000 on an experiment, would I lose sleep? No. And that's the right size for a first bet." — Daniel Vassallo

> "Good enough published beats perfectly planned stuck." — Daniel Vassallo

## Discovery Triggers

- Founders who publish first and iterate second ( Gumroad, Leanpub, YouTube as validation tools — if people pay or watch, the idea has legs)
- Start every new project with a defined "experiment budget" — if losing it wouldn't cause sleep loss, it's the right size
- Price with anchoring: start high, offer discounts, never reveal the "real" price until buyer interest is established
- Inspiration as a deliberate daily practice — Twitter/Reddit as structured ideation, not passive consumption
- Diversification across product types: courses, books, SaaS, consulting — uncorrelated revenue streams

## Validation Threshold

- Real money changes hands before significant investment (pre-orders, early access sales)
- Clear experiment budget defined upfront — success or failure is known within weeks
- Multiple independent bets running simultaneously — no single point of failure
- Willingness to kill projects that show no signal rather than doubling down out of sunk-cost fallacy

## Red Flags

- Excessive optimization before evidence of demand (polishing a product nobody has paid for)
- Emotional attachment to a single product that represents the majority of time/effort
- Experiment budgets large enough to cause real pain if lost
- Confusing activity for progress (shipping frequently but never validating with revenue)
- Portfolio of small bets that are all in the same market (not truly uncorrelated)

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Small Bets Statistician founders in the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Build small ERPNext-specific tools (apps, integrations, reports) as small bets
- Price ERPNext plugins at $29-99/month — low commitment, easy validation
- Run multiple ERPNext apps simultaneously across different niches (one for restaurants, one for agencies, one for e-commerce)
- Use ERPNext community as the validation market — publish apps on Frappe IO, measure installs and feedback
- Diversify across ERPNext verticals (manufacturing, healthcare, education, retail) rather than building one massive vertical
- Treat ERPNext integrations as small bets — build one integration, validate, move on if no signal

**High-value ERPNext small bet opportunities:**
- Single-purpose ERPNext apps (auto-backup scheduler, compliance report generator, multi-currency reconciliation)
- Industry-specific ERPNext templates (laundromat, gym, tutoring center)
- ERPNext reporting add-ons (custom P&L views, advanced inventory dashboards)
- Automation scripts for common ERPNext workflows (auto-archive, smart reconciliation)

# Alternative Solution Auditor

**Persona archetype:** The founder who validates a problem by deeply researching all non-software alternatives before building: spreadsheets, legacy software, open-source tools, agencies, manual processes, and even doing nothing. Understanding the full competitive landscape — including the status quo — is required before writing a line of code.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "Don't ask people what they want. Watch what they do." — Rob Walling

> "Going from $0 to $1 is harder than going from $1 to $1M. Most people don't appreciate that." — Sahil Lavingia

> "Look at what freelance developers are building for clients repeatedly — if you've built the same custom thing three times, productize it." — Rob Walling

## Discovery Triggers

- Deep research into what target customers are currently doing (spreadsheets, manual processes, legacy tools)
- Understanding that "doing nothing" is a valid competitor — customers may prefer the pain of the status quo
- Identifying the specific switching costs that keep customers on inadequate alternatives
- Mapping the full competitive landscape: enterprise software, spreadsheets, agencies, open-source, DIY
- Understanding why existing alternatives are inadequate (price, complexity, flexibility, reliability)
- Identifying trigger events that would force customers to change (new regulation, company growth, system failure)

## Validation Threshold

- Clear understanding of current customer behavior, not just stated preferences
- Identified specific switching costs that need to be overcome
- Known trigger events that create urgency
- Existing alternatives have measurable drawbacks (cost, time, complexity) that justify new solution
- "What almost hired instead" is understood — the alternatives customers consider

## Red Flags

- Building for a problem customers have never tried to solve
- Assuming existing alternatives are easily replaced without understanding why people use them
- Ignoring the cost of switching — even a "worse" solution with no switching cost wins
- Building a solution without understanding what customers currently pay (time, money, stress)
- Conflating "there's no tool for this" with "there's demand for this tool"

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Alternative Solution Auditors in the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Research what ERPNext users currently do for each pain point (Excel, manual processes, custom scripts)
- Understand why ERPNext's built-in features aren't sufficient for each use case
- Map the full landscape of ERPNext alternatives: Odoo, Dolibarr, Apache OFBiz, SAP Business One, QuickBooks + spreadsheets
- Identify ERPNext implementation agencies as competitors (custom development vs. packaged app)
- Understand the open-source alternative landscape (Frappe vs. custom Python apps vs. ERPNext vs. nothing)
- Find ERPNext workarounds that users have built and document why they're inadequate

**ERPNext alternative solution opportunities:**
- ERPNext apps replacing Excel-heavy workflows (spreadsheet alternatives)
- ERPNext apps replacing manual processes that ERPNext's standard modules don't cover
- ERPNext apps replacing custom scripts that companies have accumulated over years
- ERPNext apps replacing agency custom development (productized versions of common customizations)
- ERPNext apps replacing the "do nothing" option (automating things companies resigned themselves to doing manually)

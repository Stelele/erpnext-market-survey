# Speed-First Concierge

**Persona archetype:** The founder who treats speed as the primary competitive advantage. Launches in days, not months. Believes that moving fast through iteration beats moving slowly toward perfection. Personal touch and rapid response are the product, not just features.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "Your first 10 customers are worth more than your first 100. They'll teach you everything." — Andy Baldacci

> "The fastest SaaS launch is the one where you cut everything that isn't the core promise." — Pawel Brzeminski

> "Find the person who has to do this task 20 times a day and hates it. That's your customer." — (MicroConf — 5 Micro SaaS Ideas)

## Discovery Triggers

- Founders who measure time-to-launch in days, not months
- Emphasis on the simplest version that could possibly work — cut every non-essential feature
- Landing page + pre-order validation before writing code (Gumroad, Carrd, Stripe)
- "Fake door" testing — describe a feature you don't have, see if people ask for it
- Personal demos and direct founder-to-customer communication as primary feedback mechanism
- Launch with waitlist + early access model to build urgency before product is ready

## Validation Threshold

- Can get to first sale in under 30 days from concept
- Customer feedback loops measured in hours, not weeks
- Willingness to cut features that delay launch, even if they seem important
- Real customers using the product (not just email signups) within 2-4 weeks

## Red Flags

- Launch delays driven by perfectionism ("just one more feature")
- Spending more than 2 weeks on a landing page before testing with real traffic
- Waiting for "perfect" product-market fit before charging (free users are not validation)
- Analysis paralysis on tech stack, domain name, branding — decision fatigue before launch
- Conflating shipping speed with shipping slop — speed without direction is just noise

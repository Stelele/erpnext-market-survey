# System Buyer

**Core Identity:** The founder who prefers buying proven systems over building from scratch. They recognize that frameworks like EOS and Traction have been refined by thousands of businesses, and reinventing the wheel is a costly distraction.

## Motivation & Values

- **Compounding leverage:** Believes that adopting a proven system is faster than designing one from scratch, even if the off-the-shelf system isn't perfect
- **Structure as a competitive advantage:** Many bootstrapped founders fly by instrument, but the System Buyer deliberately adopts instruments and learns to fly with them
- **Job descriptions as products:** Treats the role definition process as a product — defining outputs, success metrics, and decision rights clearly
- **Avoiding reinvention:** Has the self-awareness to know they are not a systems designer by trade, and that's fine

## Behavioral Patterns

- Invests in EOS, Traction, or similar frameworks early — sometimes pays for advisors or consultants
- Builds job descriptions that read like product specs — what does this role own? what are the success metrics? what decisions does this role make independently?
- Uses the Double Diamond framework for discovery — diverge widely, then converge deliberately
- Applies EOS's L10 meetings and other cadence tools to create rhythm in the business
- Understands the stages of founder evolution: maker → founder → manager → leader → business owner, and knows each stage requires different systems

## Quotes

- "I stopped trying to invent my own management system. EOS already solved this problem."
- "The founder who tries to build everything from scratch is burning years on a solved problem."
- "Define the role before you hire for it. The job description is the spec."

## When It Breaks Down

- Buys systems without adapting them to their specific context, causing friction
- Becomes rigid in following the system rather than using it as a scaffold
- Over-invests in frameworks before the business has the volume of problems those frameworks solve

## Related Personas

- Delegation Architect — the System Buyer often adopts EOS specifically to make delegation clearer
- Documentation Obsessive — systems like EOS create natural documentation structures

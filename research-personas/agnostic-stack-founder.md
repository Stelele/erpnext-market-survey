# Agnostic Stack Founder

**Core Identity:** The founder who chooses technology based on what works best with AI tooling and future-proof constraints, not personal preference — recognizing that the best tech stack in the AI age is one that AI can reason about.

## Motivation & Values

- **AI compatibility as a selection criterion:** A language or framework that AI understands well is more valuable than one the founder prefers personally
- **Contextual knowledge matters:** AI can help with stacks it's trained on, but the founder still needs enough technical understanding to guide and validate the AI's output
- **Taste without attachment:** Has strong opinions about what good software looks like but remains flexible about the implementation language
- **Future-proof over comfort:** Would rather use a slightly less preferred technology that integrates well with AI tools than a beloved stack that AI struggles with

## Behavioral Patterns

- Evaluates new technologies not just by personal preference but by how well AI tools can assist with that stack
- Maintains enough technical understanding to catch AI hallucinations and bad suggestions
- Experiments with AI coding assistants (Cursor, Copilot) and adjusts their stack based on what works
- Treats the tech stack decision as a leverage decision — where can AI amplify their efforts most?
- Watches for the shift: the best tool 5 years ago may not be the best tool today given AI integration

## Quotes

- "I used to have strong opinions about my stack. Now I have strong opinions about my output, and I'll use whatever the AI works best with."
- "AI knows PHP well enough to help. But does it know your obscure framework?"
- "The founder doesn't need to write every line. They need to know enough to tell the AI when it's wrong."

## When It Breaks Down

- Chases every new AI-compatible tool, creating constant context-switching overhead
- Over-relies on AI suggestions without sufficient technical grounding to evaluate them
- Chooses technology based on AI capability alone, ignoring community, ecosystem, and long-term viability

## Related Personas

- Ownership Paradox Navigator — both think carefully about tech constraints; Agnostic Stack focuses on AI compatibility while Ownership Paradox focuses on platform independence and sellability
- Tool Hoarder Purger — both have a relationship with technology choices; Agnostic Stack chooses proactively while Tool Hoarder Purger cleans up reactively

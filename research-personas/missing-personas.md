# Missing Personas & Unread Source Material

Personas we identified but haven't yet mined from source transcripts, plus transcripts that would deepen existing personas.

## Additional Personas to Investigate

### 1. Freelance Pivot Builder
**Who they are:** Founders who started as freelancers/consultants and are looking for the "productization moment" — when client demand becomes predictable enough to build software. **Needed:** Rob Walling "33 Minutes" (full), Matt Inglot pre-sale episode

### 2. Competition Monitor
**Who they are:** Founders who obsess over competitor landscape — tracking what competitors build, price, and ship. Uses competitive intel to inform product decisions without becoming paralyzed. **Needed:** Rob Walling "7 Proven Ways" (full)

### 3. Vertical SaaS Specialist
**Who they are:** Founders who deliberately choose industry-specific niches (dentists, lawyers, HVAC, gyms) over horizontal tools. Understands that vertical SaaS has less competition and higher willingness to pay. **Needed:** MicroConf "5 Micro SaaS Ideas" (full speaker details)

### 4. Tool Hoarder Purger
**Who they are:** Founders who recognize that too many tools create complexity, cost, and cognitive load. Actively audits and reduces their software stack. **Needed:** TBF #370 (Systems That Work While You Don't — full transcript)

### 5. Async-First Operator
**Who they are:** Founders who deliberately design their businesses around asynchronous communication — no real-time meetings, no Slack pressure, no synchronous dependency. **Needed:** TBF #355 (partial read, could explore deeper)

### 6. Pricing Psychologist
**Who they are:** Founders who treat pricing as the most powerful product lever — anchoring, tier design, value-based pricing, and the psychology of what customers perceive as fair. **Needed:** Rob Walling "33 Minutes" (full), TBF #413 (Moat Architect deep dive — already read, could expand)

### 7. Customer Interview Specialist
**Who they are:** Founders who treat every customer conversation as a research opportunity. Deep expertise in discovery questioning, "jobs to be done" framing, and extracting signal from customer noise. **Needed:** The Bootstrapped Founder #419 (Missing Piece in Validation — partial read, could go deeper)

### 8. Platform Depender
**Who they are:** Founders who deliberately build on top of established platforms (Frappe, WordPress, Shopify) rather than building from scratch. Leverage platform distribution and trust. **Needed:** Could be synthesized from existing ERPNext/Frappe source material

### 9. Niche Aggregator
**Who they are:** Founders who identify fragmented niches and consolidate them — serving multiple small markets with one product through configuration rather than customization. **Needed:** TBF #378 (partial read)

### 10. Retention Obsessive
**Who they are:** Founders who treat churn as the primary growth lever. Every feature, pricing decision, and customer interaction is evaluated for its impact on retention. **Needed:** Rob Walling "Break Through the 7 SaaS Plateaus" (full, already summarized)

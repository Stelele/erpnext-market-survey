# Founder Fit Analyzer

**Persona archetype:** The founder who validates three-way fit: market meets founder, product meets market, and product meets founder. Rejects the idea that any good market or any good idea is sufficient. The intersection of personal passion, market demand, and practical capability is where sustainable businesses emerge.

## Lighthouse Quote

> "The skills that got you to $5K MRR are not the skills that will get you to $50K MRR. You have to level up." — Patrick McKenzie

> "Find people who are two levels above you and learn from them." — Patrick McKenzie

## Discovery Triggers

- Deliberate assessment of personal skills, passions, and constraints before choosing a market
- Understanding that market-entry requires not just a good idea but founder-market alignment
- Identifying the "founder bottleneck" — the founder as the limiting factor in growth
- Building a "personal board of directors" for honest feedback and perspective
- Deliberate skill development: one skill gap each quarter, deliberately practiced
- Succession planning: documenting every process so the business doesn't depend solely on the founder

## Validation Threshold

- Founder has genuine interest/passion in the chosen market (sustains through hard times)
- Founder has or can acquire the skills needed to build and sell in that market
- Market is large enough to support a business the founder actually wants to build
- Founder's constraints (time, money, location) match the business requirements

## Red Flags

- Choosing a market based purely on revenue potential without founder fit
- Ignoring personal sustainability — building a business that makes money but drains the founder
- Skipping the founder-market fit assessment because the idea seems good
- Conflating personal interest with market demand
- Ignoring skill gaps and assuming execution will bridge them

## ERPNext Ecosystem Lens

Founder Fit Analyzers entering the ERPNext ecosystem would:

- Assess whether they have genuine passion for business operations, ERP systems, and workflow automation
- Evaluate if their technical skills (Python, Frappe Framework) match ERPNext development needs
- Determine if they enjoy the ERPNext community and want to be part of it long-term
- Consider whether ERPNext's technical architecture fits their preferred way of building
- Validate that ERPNext's growth trajectory aligns with the founder's goals (ERPNext is growing globally)
- Assess if the founder's target market overlaps with ERPNext's core user base (SMEs, growing companies)

**ERPNext founder fit questions:**
- Do you enjoy debugging complex business logic in Python?
- Do you find satisfaction in making complex operations simple through software?
- Are you comfortable with the Frappe Framework's particular way of building apps?
- Do you want to serve the ERPNext community long-term or just extract short-term value?
- Does your target customer overlap with ERPNext's existing user base?
